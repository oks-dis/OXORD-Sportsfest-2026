SPORTFEST 2025

📢 ANNOUNCEMENT

To all participants:

We would like to remind everyone that this is a fair contest. Therefore, it is strictly prohibited for facilitators or any skilled individuals outside the team to assist in creating layouts, editing videos, or completing other tasks that may give unfair advantage to any group.

This contest is meant to highlight your own creativity, teamwork, and effort. Winning will be far more meaningful when it comes from your own hands and ideas. Let us uphold the values of fairness, integrity, and honesty throughout this event.

"And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men; Knowing that of the Lord ye shall receive the reward of the inheritance: for ye serve the Lord Christ."
— Colossians 3:23-24 (KJV)

▲ Remember: God sees everything you do. You may hide from people, but you can never hide from Him.

Your own work will always be more rewarding, and the joy of creating something with your own effort is far greater than relying on others.

✨ Let’s keep the spirit of fairness alive and make this contest a true celebration of talent and teamwork!
